# SIWES assessment criteria (source: SIWES Assessment Criteria, 16-09-2025)

The student is graded out of 100 across three components. Every file this skill produces
exists to earn marks in one of these rows. Read this file before planning any deliverable.

## 1. Log Book: 30 marks

| Sub-criterion | Marks | What the examiner looks for | How this skill earns it |
|---|---|---|---|
| No. of weeks on SIWES | 5 | Every week of the placement is present and filled | One entry per scheduled working day, every week heading present |
| Illustrations (graphs, sketches) | 5 | Diagrams, circuit/network sketches, charts that support the entries | One numbered Figure slot per week with a specific description, plus rendered diagrams where facts allow |
| Neatness | 5 | Consistent, legible, ordered, dated | Fixed table layout, DD-Mon-YYYY dates, supervisor sign-off line each week |
| Technical content | 10 | Specific tools, steps, components, results; not "assisted staff" | 45–90 word entries with action, tools, validation |
| Relevance to course of study | 5 | Work clearly connects to Computer Engineering | At least 70% of days are hardware, software, networking, embedded or electronics work; remarks column ties each day to a concept |

## 2. Training Report: 30 marks

| Sub-criterion | Marks | What the examiner looks for | How this skill earns it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conformity with prescribed format | 5 | Correct sections, title page, ToC, professional layout | Preliminary pages, Chapters 1–5, references, appendices (default structure in report-structure.md) |
| Technical content | 10 | Depth: procedures, tools, specs, results, case studies | Chapter 3 ~3,000 words, numbered procedures, 2–3 worked case studies |
| Linkage of report to theory | 5 | Named concepts applied to named tasks | Chapter 4 maps task → concept → application, with two worked examples |
| Originality of report | 5 | The writing reflects this student's own experience | Built from the student's own chat/logbook; human-voice rules from writing-style.md |
| Conclusions / recommendations | 5 | Follow from the evidence; concrete and addressed to someone | Chapter 5 with recommendations split by audience |

## 3. Oral Presentation: 40 marks

| Sub-criterion | Marks | ~Share of talk | Slides and oral script that carry it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Summary of work-experience | 15 | ~40% | Company/role slide + 4–5 work-stream slides with photos + 1 case study |
| Linkage of work to theory | 5 | ~12% | Theory-linkage table slide + one worked-example callout slide |
| Linkage to future studies | 5 | ~12% | Future studies slide |
| Professional competence gained | 5 | ~12% | Skills gained slide (before/after format) |
| Quality of presentation technique | 10 | whole talk | Action titles, one idea per slide, photos, timed oral scripts |

## Not defined by the criteria document

- The exact report layout is set by the student's department. Ask for their template; never assume.
- Page counts, referencing style and presentation length vary by institution. If not given,
  use defaults in report-structure.md and state which defaults were used.
